Chương 1: Foundational Biomechanical Principles in Tennis & Movement

Biomechanical Principles of Tennis Technique: Using Science to Improve Your Strokes — Duane Knudson, Ph.D.

Elite athletic execution relies on rigorous physical biomechanics. The Kinetic Chain demonstrates that striking power originates not from the distal upper limb, but from ground reaction forces channeled sequentially through legs, pelvis, and trunk before accelerating into the racquet.

Any break in kinetic sequencing—such as insufficient hip drive or premature trunk flexion—forces the shoulder and forearm to compensate, causing severe overuse pathologies like lateral epicondylitis and rotator cuff tears.

This Western scientific model perfectly mirrors classical internal martial aphorisms: 'Rooted in the feet, issued through the thighs, controlled by the waist, and expressed through the fingers.'
