Chương 3: Biomechanics of Groundstrokes: Modern Forehand and Backhand

Biomechanical Principles of Tennis Technique: Using Science to Improve Your Strokes — Duane Knudson, Ph.D.

The modern forehand has evolved decisively toward open-stance setups, storing massive rotational torque within the abdominal obliques and releasing instantaneous angular acceleration.

To generate heavy topspin, the racquet head descends below ball trajectory and sweeps sharply upward through contact, exploiting the Magnus effect to create high clearance and vicious downward dip.

The two-handed backhand provides exceptional trunk stability and return-of-serve defense, whereas the single-handed backhand offers superior reach and angular leverage.
