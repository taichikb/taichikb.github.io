Chương 4: Volleys, Net Play, and Movement Mechanics

Biomechanical Principles of Tennis Technique: Using Science to Improve Your Strokes — Duane Knudson, Ph.D.

The split-step represents the fundamental precursor to all athletic court court-coverage. Landing lightly on the forefeet precisely as the opponent impacts the ball pre-tenses the calves for explosive lateral push-off.

Volleys demand compact blocking mechanics rather than sweeping backswings, utilizing forward linear step momentum to redirect ball pace with pinpoint touch.

Deceleration and change-of-direction maneuvers impose heavy eccentric demands upon the quadriceps, necessitating rigorous conditioning to safeguard the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL).
